01 / Initiative

One initiative, many subtracks

The Global InferenceNet Initiative, hosted by NTU GIFTS, aims to build shared infrastructure for training, evaluating and applying AI agents in economics, finance, business and the social sciences. The Challenge is where it meets the community.

  • Aim 1

    Tasks and databases

    To build large-scale collections of real research tasks, analyses, data-and-code workflows and research designs.

  • Aim 2

    Benchmarks and competitions

    To test independently whether AI agents can plan, carry out, interpret and audit professional-level analytical work.

  • Aim 3

    Training and applications

    To provide shared infrastructure for training and deploying agents, with educational resources and tools for analysts and consultants.

02 / Subtracks

Challenge subtracks

Each subtrack evaluates agent workflows through its tasks, deliverables and scoring criteria.

Subtrack 01Dev100 · 100 tasks

Econometric replication

Reproduce empirical findings from real research data. An AI agent writes and runs code to recover a published coefficient, its standard error and its p-value.

Tasks
Dev100: 100 econometric replication tasks.
Input
The research data and the exact instruction the model receives.
Output
A result file with three numbers: the coefficient, the standard error and the p-value.
Evaluation
An independent evaluator checks the three numbers against reference results that the model never sees during the run.
Results
Explore Dev100 / v10 development results on the Leaderboard.

Subtrack 02Finance & HR

Business Analysis & Deliverables

Complex business scenarios contributed by Prof. Liwen Zhang’s team at Shanghai University of Finance and Economics. Agents work across multiple source documents to produce reports and spreadsheets, assessed against task-specific rubrics.

Evaluation
An independent judge scores the files against a task-specific rubric that the agent never sees.

03 / Timeline

From open exploration to awards

Five stages, from task exploration to independent evaluation and awards.

  1. 01Now

    Open exploration

    Explore the research data source, the task walkthroughs and the historical leaderboards.

  2. 02

    Registration and pilot

    Register your interest in a pilot of the evaluation workflow.

  3. 03

    Challenge window

    Participants submit their solutions during a fixed, announced period.

  4. 04

    Independent evaluation

    Every submission is evaluated under the same settings.

  5. 05

    Results and awards

    Results are published and the awards are announced.
