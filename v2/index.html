01The Metacase

From flat text to a living episode.

Standard benchmarks keep only the prompt and the final score, discarding how a problem was framed, what failed, and why a strategy worked. A Metacase records the full episode across Economics, Finance, and Social Science — and slices it at a strict cutoff into an executable Metacase Challenge task where Models and Harnesses run, get verified, and leave reusable experience.

  1. Ch 1Raw evidence & cutoffFreeze a real episode at the decision cutoff — raw papers, filings, ledgers, and tables, with hindsight stripped out — to form a leak-free Challenge task.
  2. Ch 2Priors & strategy trade-offsGround the problem in institutional rules and prior practice, weigh competing candidate routes, set aside flawed paths, and commit to a strategy.
  3. Ch 3Model × Harness trajectoryInside a reproducible Harbor container, a Model + Harness executes step-by-step tool calls — writing code, querying data, and building auditable deliverables.
  4. Ch 4Verified outcome & rubricCode verifiers and multi-dimensional rubrics grade the deliverable against ground truth, separating reasoning errors from environment faults.
  5. Ch 5Reflection & experience reuseDistill why the trajectory succeeded or broke — turning one graded trial into reusable experience for human researchers and self-evolving harnesses.
  6. SignatureSolution signatureClosed again, the Metacase carries its five-stage solution signature on the fore-edge — the key for retrieving episodes solved the same way across fields.

03The network

A reusable network of Metacases.

  1. Field by fieldEconomics, finance and social science each hold their own Metacases, with their own data, people and institutions.
  2. Linked by solution pathWhen cases in different fields were solved the same way, the network links them.
  3. TransferAgents and people follow those links to reuse what worked in another field; every reviewed case joins the network.
  4. The foundationA foundation for experience-based AI.Every reviewed case joins the network, and the next problem starts from more experience.

∴Global Metacase Initiative

Metacase Challenge

The initiative is to build a reusable network of Metacases — end-to-end representations of problem-solving, decision episodes, or task completion, linking the original context and raw evidence to the approaches considered, prior human or institutional practices, reasoning, candidate strategies, actions taken, subsequent outcomes, interpretations, and lessons learned.

The goal is to let AI systems, as well as humans, retrieve and reason over not just similar facts, but similar problem-solving and decision structures, so they can learn how problems were previously understood, solved, and refined. MetacaseNet, the collection of metacases, can therefore serve as a foundation for experience-based AI in economics, finance, healthcare, policy, law, and other social science and business domains.

    05The Metacase Challenge

    Fair runs, full trajectories.

    The Challenge is the first pipeline into the Metacase network. Four rules keep every run comparable and every trajectory worth reviewing.

    1. End-to-end tasksAgents work from the original evidence to a finished deliverable, not a single answer.
    2. Hidden referencesReference results and rubrics stay hidden from agents until evaluation.
    3. Same tasks for everyoneEvery entry in a track faces the same data, materials and evaluation.
    4. Full trajectoriesEach run keeps its reasoning, actions and outcome, so the result can be inspected.
