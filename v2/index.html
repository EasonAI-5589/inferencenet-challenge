How both challenges work

From requirements to independent evaluation

Challenge 1 · Econometric replicationChallenge 2 · Business Analysis & Deliverables
01 / 04

Workflow

Challenge 1

Inspect the data, construct the required sample and variables, implement the estimator, then debug and revise using execution feedback.

Challenge 2

Read source documents, reconcile records and rules, perform calculations, then reopen and revise the report and workbook.

02 / 04

Core difficulty

Challenge 1

Translate controls, interactions, fixed effects and clustered errors into code. A program can run successfully yet estimate the wrong quantity.

Challenge 2

Resolve inconsistent evidence and apply professional rules. Calculations, explanations and recommendations must agree across documents.

03 / 04

Deliverable

Challenge 1

A result file containing the target coefficient, standard error and p-value, computed by the agent’s analysis program.

Challenge 2

A Word report and Excel workbook with traceable evidence, calculations and explicit decisions, following the task’s format requirements.

04 / 04

Independent evaluation

Challenge 1

Compare the submitted statistics with withheld reference results, examining numerical agreement, effect direction and significance.

Challenge 2

Check the files against source evidence and task-specific rubrics, scoring correctness, traceability and violations of required rules.

03 / Our aims

What InferenceNet Challenge builds

Four ways InferenceNet Challenge advances the Initiative: community collaboration, challenging problems, standard evaluation, and model training and improvement.

01

Open-source community

Connect researchers, developers and domain experts to share code, tools, methods and results. Help teams build on each other’s work, solve problems together and accelerate research and applications.

Collaborate with us→
02

Challenging problems

Collect demanding problems from research and professional practice, with data, task materials, deliverables and evaluation criteria. Build reusable tasks that test agents’ ability to complete complex work.

Explore the challenges→
03

Standard evaluation framework

Standardize task interfaces, execution records and independent evaluation protocols. Verify results against task-specific criteria so models and harnesses can be assessed reproducibly and compared under consistent conditions.

Explore the evaluation setup→
04

Model training and improvement

Use task data, verified trajectories and failure feedback to study model training and harness optimization. Test improvements on new tasks, turning evaluation findings into evidence for better systems.

Explore training and improvement research→
04 / Get involved

Build with us

We welcome benchmark tasks, domain partners and collaborators working on models, harnesses and evaluation.

Bring your domain expertise, a new challenge, an agent implementation or evaluation ideas. Work with the community to turn challenging problems into reusable tasks, reproducible evaluation and better agents.
