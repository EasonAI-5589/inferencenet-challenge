    How to read this page

    A configuration explorer, not a leaderboard.

    • Models and harnesses come from the existing Leaderboard and Harness pages. Gear lists capabilities harnesses commonly expose; availability depends on the harness configuration.
    • Measured results are shown only for a model and harness pair that was actually run: Dev100 v10 (100 development tasks, four harnesses) or Selected_1000 (1,000 tasks, single model or Model + DeepAgents). Failed and unknown runs stay in the denominator.
    • Gear does not change any number. Every other combination is not yet measured — that is what the Challenge is for.
