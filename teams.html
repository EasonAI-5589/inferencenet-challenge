Nanyang Technological University, Singapore

NTU GIFTS

Global Institute of Finance, Technology, and Society

Founding Director · Lin William Cong ↗

A university-wide institute jointly anchored by Nanyang Business School and CCDS. GIFTS brings together research in AI, finance and the digital economy, connecting academic work with industry collaboration and policy.

  • AI & the digital economy
  • Financial technology
  • Economic simulation
Related initiatives

Global InferenceNet develops benchmarks and training infrastructure for research agents. The Economic World Models Initiative studies simulated economies, markets and interactions between human and AI agents.

Visit GIFTS ↗Research initiatives ↗

The University of Hong Kong · CAMO

HKU AI-Agents Lab

Lab for AI-Agents in Business and Economics

Lab Directors · Ye Luo & Xin Tong

Part of HKU's Centre for AI, Management and Organization (CAMO), the lab develops AI agents for business and economics. Its research spans multi-agent systems, specialist agents with domain expertise, and the effects of AI on people, firms and organizations.

  • Multi-agent systems
  • Domain-specific agents
  • AI & organizations
Related project

Econometric AI develops agents for econometric research and education, including analysis planning, code generation and execution. The lab's project page links to the public Econometrics-Agent code and InferenceNet dataset.
