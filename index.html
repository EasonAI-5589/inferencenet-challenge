InferenceNet Challenge

AI Agents for Business, Finance and Social Science

Global InferenceNet Initiative builds shared infrastructure for training, evaluating, and deploying AI agents in economics, finance, business, and the social sciences. InferenceNet Challenge is a series of open challenges for AI agents, with each challenge forming a subtrack with its own tasks and evaluation criteria.

01 / Inference Challenge

Inference Challenge

Two subtracks explore different kinds of agent work: reproducing research findings and completing professional tasks. Each has its own tasks and evaluation criteria.

  • Subtrack 01 · Dev100 · 100 tasks

    Econometric replication

    Reproduce empirical findings from real research data. This subtrack will open-source the existing 100-task Dev100 set; its task package and rules will be announced with the release.

    Evaluation checks the reproduced statistics against reference results.

    Explore Dev100 results →

  • Subtrack 02 · Finance & HR demos

    Professional tasks

    Complex business scenarios contributed by Prof. Liwen Zhang’s team at Shanghai University of Finance and Economics. Agents work across multiple source documents to produce reports and spreadsheets, assessed against task-specific rubrics.

    • Finance · Month-end close reviewPrepare a July closing remediation plan and review workpapers.
    • HR · Training and procurement acceptanceAssess cold-chain warehouse supervisor training and supplier deliverables in a fictional scenario.

02 / A real case

Finding the month variable

One recorded GPT-5.5 task shows how execution feedback changes the analysis. DeepAgents is the harness used in this example.

Task 0011 · Journal of Economic History

How does socio-economic conflict relate to economic policy uncertainty in Spain?

Reproduce the specified regression for 1905–1945, with three controls and standard errors clustered by month.

data_np.dta4,550 rows · 38 columnsWhat is in the data? →

GPT-5.5 · Model

The first program stops

The model generates a program that searches for familiar month-column names. The actual month index is named tid.

The program cannot identify it, so execution fails before producing a result.

1 model call

GPT-5.5 + DeepAgents

Inspect, execute, reproduce

The model inspects the columns, identifies the monthly index, and checks the sample and regression. DeepAgents runs its code and returns the output at each step.

The final program uses 977 observations and reproduces all three target statistics.

4 model calls · 3 tool executions

Two separate runs of the same task. The final program is replayed and checked independently.

04 / Research

What can we learn from these runs?

We aim to use replication results and execution feedback to improve how models and harnesses work together.

  • 01

    Model–Harness Coupling

    Study which model and harness combinations work well, and how planning, tool use and feedback affect replication.

    Harness: what it is, three taken apart →

  • 02

    Self-Evolving Harnesses

    An outer loop proposes, tests and keeps harness versions using feedback from the harness’s own runs. Compare three methods from recent papers, ModularRSI, Self-Harness and Meta-Harness, and see our first ModularRSI-style development study.

    Self-evolving: concept and methods →
    Our first ModularRSI-style study →

  • 03

    Trajectory-Based Post-Training

    Explore validated execution trajectories as training signals for models, including distillation and reinforcement learning.

These are research directions we hope to pursue with collaborators. Explore the research directions →
