Beyond code.
Back to evidence.

Running an analysis is one step. Reproducing the finding is the test. InferenceNet connects empirical research tasks, data and statistical results to study what AI can reliably do.

About InferenceNet

Can AI reproduce empirical research?

The InferenceNet Challenge focuses on how effectively AI models and agents can perform empirical research and reproduce published findings. Building on the InferenceNet benchmark, it brings together social science and AI around a shared question: how can we make automated analysis more reliable?

The research benchmark considers both executable analysis and the reproduction of statistical results, including coefficients, standard errors and p-values. The challenge's evaluation protocol, task selection and participation details will be published here as they are confirmed.
