Global Metacase Initiative

Metacase Challenge

We present the Metacase Challenge（Metacase Challenge） — an open benchmark and research arena built around reusable Metacases across Economics, Finance, and Social Science. Each Metacase records an end-to-end problem-solving episode: from original context and raw evidence to prior domain practice, candidate strategies, Model × Harness trajectories, verified outcomes, and reusable lessons.

The goal is for both AI systems and researchers to retrieve and reason over similar problem-solving and decision structures across fields, turning validated episodes into a foundation for experience-based AI.

01The Metacase

A Metacase is one episode, recorded end to end.

A Metacase is the full record of one episode of problem-solving, decision-making, or task completion — from the raw evidence to the reusable lesson. Each Challenge task is drawn from a curated, representative long-horizon complex problem at the point of decision: the agent sees only what was known then, and the references used for scoring stay hidden from it.

For an agent this is a Model + Harness run: the model decides, the harness carries out the tool calls and keeps the log.Every step and tool call, in orderThe model decides, the harness executesRead · Plan · Execute · Deliver

An outcome may be a success, a partial success or a failure; all three are kept.Checked against a reference or rubricPass, partial or fail: all are kept

02Retrieval

Retrieve experience by how a problem was solved.

Every Metacase carries a solution signature: how the evidence was framed, which options were weighed, the order of actions, and how the outcome was checked. Searching by that structure finds cases worked the same way across fields — providing a better way to aggregate and organize agent trajectories into reusable experience.

  1. Topic matchA topic search for this credit decision returns more credit cases. Laid over its signature, their paths split at most stages: they weighed evidence, acted and checked in other ways. A shared topic does not mean the experience applies.
  2. Solution matchA signature search returns cases from economics and social science whose stages correspond: evidence organised alike, options compared, the same order of action, a similar check. Each ringed stage is a step that lines up; the slips are a schematic of that comparison.
  3. ReuseA person or an agent reads the matched cases for their evidence, trade-offs, outcomes and limits, decides what is worth carrying over, and tests it on the new task. A match informs the next attempt; it does not supply the answer.

03The network

A reusable network of Metacases.

Metacases are linked wherever their solution structures correspond, within a field and across fields. Each reviewed case adds a record and its links, so a new problem can start from related experience instead of from scratch.

  1. Field by fieldEach field grows from its own cases: economics, finance and social science, each with its own data, institutions and practice.
  2. Linked by solution pathWhere cases in different fields were worked through the same way, their signatures correspond and the network links them.
  3. TransferPeople and agents follow those links to see how a comparable problem was handled elsewhere, then test whether the approach carries over.
  4. The foundationA foundation for experience-based AI.That is the aim: as reviewed cases accumulate, new problems start from more recorded experience. The first fields are economics, finance and social science; healthcare, policy and law belong to the longer-term vision.

    06Participation workflow

    How to participate in the challenge.

    What a participant does: take a case from one of the three tracks, run a model and its harness on it end to end, have the deliverable checked against references the agent never sees, and turn what happened into something others can inspect and reuse.

    1. 1 · Choose a caseThe participant takes a case from a track and opens it: the task goal, the evidence available at the decision point and its constraints. Every entry in a track works from the same materials and is scored the same way.
    2. 2 · Configure Model + HarnessThey pick a model and the harness that runs it. The model reasons and decides; the harness supplies the tools, the workflow and the run settings, so the same model can behave differently in a different harness.
    3. 3 · Execute and recordThe agent reads the materials, plans, calls tools and writes files until it delivers end to end. Every step goes into the trajectory, so the whole run can be traced afterwards.
    4. 4 · Verify and diagnoseThe deliverable goes to an independent check against reference results and rubrics the agent never saw during the run. A result can be a pass, a partial pass or a failure, and the trajectory shows where it went wrong.
    5. 5 · ContributeWhat the run yields can be contributed in three forms: a Metacase, the structured record of the case; the Model & Harness, packaged so the run can be repeated; or a Failure Case, a reproducible failure with its diagnosis (a low score alone is not one). Contribute one or more, separately from the choice of track.
