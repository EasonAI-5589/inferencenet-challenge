01 / Agent workflow

From the task goal to a checked submission

The agent needs to plan, use tools, inspect evidence and revise its work. The outline below describes how these tasks can be approached; the pilot results show what the first runs actually delivered.

Observed in the HR pilot

Read rules and evidence → write and run a Python script → inspect results → edit the script and rerun → check the Word and Excel files.

These tool checks and revisions did not prevent the attendance errors and behavioral misclassification documented below.

The finance package combines public references with synthetic business records. The HR package uses synthetic materials. Both task briefs require using the supplied materials.

FinanceFIN-T1-003 July Month-End Close: Corrections and Review PlanningResolve accounting issues and prepare a group close review supported by a calculation workbook. 55input files

Materials

Ledgers, internal policies and correspondence, accounting standards and annual reports.

18 CSV · 28 DOCX · 4 PDF · 4 HTML · 1 LOG

Deliverables

A Word review document and an Excel workbook with five non-empty worksheets, retaining the formulas needed to trace the calculations.

DOCX + XLSX

What review checks

Accounting treatment, hedge calculations, partial disposal analysis, traceability and consistency between the narrative and workbook.

25 scored criteria, including 5 penalties · 2 red lines

What the pilot exposed

The agent produced all five worksheets without formulas, failing the traceability check. It also failed to distinguish a 70% economic hedging target from the accounting hedge ratio and omitted parts of the disposal analysis.

Human resourcesHR-T3-114 Cold-Chain Supervisor Training: Impact Review and Procurement AcceptanceAssess training impact and recommend acceptance and payment for three procurement packages. 17input files

Materials

Training and attendance records, operational results, procurement evidence and supporting documents.

8 CSV · 5 XLSX · 4 DOCX

Deliverables

An Excel analysis workbook and a Word memorandum covering training outcomes, package acceptance and the allocation of payments.

XLSX + DOCX

What review checks

Population scope, attendance, center-level impact estimates, evidence-unit acceptance and payment categories. Limited opportunity to demonstrate a skill must be distinguished from poor performance.

34 scored criteria, including 6 penalties · No red lines

What the pilot exposed

The agent omitted the source index, reported 80/81 attendance instead of 82/83 and used an average across centers instead of center-level net changes. A penalty was triggered when it marked everyone with insufficient opportunity as failing the behavioral standard.

03 / How review works

Independent review after submission

The judge evaluates the final submission separately from the agent’s own checks. It examines the output files against the rubric and uses the supplied materials to verify their claims.

Rubric reward · 0–1

Weighted positive credit − weighted violations

divided by the total positive weight

The result is clipped to [0, 1]. Penalties do not increase the denominator. A confirmed red-line violation makes the finance reward zero.

Multiple dimensions
Professional standards, correct conclusions, calculations, instruction following, analysis, factual fidelity and safety.
Reference outputs
Used to calibrate structure and scale, not as an answer to copy. A different valid solution should not lose credit for differing from the reference.
Scoring failures
verifier_error marks an unavailable score that needs reevaluation, not a valid zero. It must be reported separately from scored outcomes.

Finance uses binary and five-level criteria; five-level ratings are normalized to [0, 1]. HR uses binary criteria. Total positive weight: finance 172; HR 106.

Before formal evaluation

The reference also needs calibration

The HR reference scored 0.783. Its three unmet criteria reveal two issues to resolve before this becomes a formal subtrack.

  1. Clarify cross-file requirements

    Two criteria were read as requiring accepted evidence-unit counts in both the workbook and memorandum. The reference gave the counts only in the workbook. The rubric wording and reference need to agree on what each file must state.

  2. Define formula recalculation

    Thirteen formulas in the reference payment sheet had no cached values. Reading the cached results returned blanks and caused one criterion to fail. Recalculation and formula-reading rules need to be standardized.
